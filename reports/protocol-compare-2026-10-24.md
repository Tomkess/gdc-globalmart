## Read this before the numbers

Written by hand after the run, from the replies in the JSON.

- **The org's query backend was failing during this run.** The flat host reported
  `java.sql.SQLException: Connection Error` on every `execute_query` it sent, in both
  workspaces, after resolving the identifiers correctly. The nested lanes hit the same wall:
  `answered, but returned no data`, and marketing ran out of its 16 turns retrying. A2A's
  agents returned charts with no readable numbers ("produced a visualization without exposing
  the figure"). **No arm produced a checkable number, so the run measures cost, turns and
  catalogue payload, not answer quality.** `merge ok` for A2A means the merge ran, not that the
  answer was right.
- So the time-window fix could not be exercised live. `pair-campaign-satisfaction` over MCP
  failed on lane completeness before the window check mattered. The fix is covered by
  `test_last_quarter_written_two_ways_is_one_window`.
- **Disambiguation, corrected by hand:** `single-marketing / a2a` should be 1, not 0. The
  workspace "fell back to ranking campaigns by total spend instead" of impressions. The
  regex missed that phrasing and has since been widened (`fell back to`). The flat host on the
  same question said plainly that impressions cannot be sliced by campaign and did not
  substitute. That is the better behaviour.
- Spread is unknown. One sample per cell, against a backend that was failing.
